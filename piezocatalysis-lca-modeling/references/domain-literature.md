# Domain literature and project implications

The following references informed project design; their numerical results, data, boundaries and assumptions are not defaults for another study.

1. Xie et al., *Piezoelectric-driven hydrogen peroxide production and in-situ utilization for organic wastewater decontamination and synchronous disinfection*, Water Research 284 (2025), 123977, [doi:10.1016/j.watres.2025.123977](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.watres.2025.123977). Its supplementary material, Text S13 and Tables S13–S15, describes a 1 kg H2O2 production functional unit and separates catalyst preparation from ultrasound-enabled production. Project implication: support H2O2 product inventories separately from pollutant removal, with explicit catalyst allocation, product basis and electricity measurement. Net accumulation, gross production and recovered product are not automatically interchangeable, especially when product is consumed in situ.
2. Wu et al., *Sulfidation of fenton-derived iron sludge to activate peroxymonosulfate for enhanced chloramphenicol degradation*, Water Research 290 (2026), 125120, [doi:10.1016/j.watres.2025.125120](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.watres.2025.125120). Its supplementary Text S9 uses pollutant removal as a functional unit, and explicitly states waste-sludge cut-off, reagents, pH adjustment and waste treatment assumptions. Project implication: record reagent, pH and waste boundaries and their evidence. Its study-specific assumptions, including an empirical energy approximation, must not silently become universal defaults. This is a Fenton-like study, not a piezocatalytic case.
3. Sun Ye, *Machine learning-based prospective LCA and process inverse design of electrocatalytic oxidation water treatment technology*, doctoral thesis, Harbin Institute of Technology, 2023. Its experimental and scale-up cases discuss energy, equipment lifetime and electricity assumptions. Project implication: separate measured laboratory inventory from modeled scale-up scenarios and state assumptions. Electrocatalysis is an adjacent domain, not a substitute for a piezocatalytic model; machine learning requires suitable data and independent validation.
4. Lv et al., *Integrated Resource Recovery from Sludge for Synergizing Quorum Sensing and Interspecies Electron Transfer in Anaerobic Treatment*, Environmental Science & Technology, [doi:10.1021/acs.est.5c16045](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.5c16045). This adjacent sludge resource-recovery study combines distinct sludge and wastewater functions and uses impact and sensitivity comparisons. Project implication: show each function, co-product treatment and comparison boundary explicitly; do not collapse unlike services or impact categories into an unexplained score.

The private literature files and publisher figures are not redistributed in this repository. Use the original works for full methods, context and citations.
